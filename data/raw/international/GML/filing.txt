                      Geology         Deposit type, geological setting and style of          The Penouta deposit lies in the Central Iberian Zone of the Iberian Massif
                                      mineralisation.                                        incorporating the north-western part of the “Ollo de Sapo” Formation. Regional
                                                                                             geology comprises the Viana do Bolo Series, including the Covelo orthogneisses,
                                                                                             the Ollo de Sapo Formation, and the Penouta alkaline granite.
                                                                                             The Penouta deposit comprises two principal lithologies:
                                                                                                  1.   Alkaline leucogranite – fine to medium-grained, equigranular and
                                                                                                       leucocratic, composed mainly of quartz, K-feldspar, albite and
                                                                                                       muscovite, and hosting cassiterite (60–800µm) and Nb-Ta oxides (20–
                                                                                                       360µm) as disseminations and aggregates.
                                                                                                  2.   Greisen – medium-grained white-mica–quartz assemblage with only
                                                                                                       sparse cassiterite and Nb-Ta oxides (≤360 µm); and quartz veins,
                                                                                                       occurring as sigmoidal, lenticular or tabular bodies 30 cm to 2 m thick,
                                                                                                       typically hosted in augen gneiss or locally in the leucogranite, and
                                                                                                       characterised by muscovite-rich selvages and abundant coarse
                                                                                                       cassiterite.
